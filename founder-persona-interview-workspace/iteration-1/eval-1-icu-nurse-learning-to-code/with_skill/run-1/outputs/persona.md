## Summary

Marcus is a 7-year UCSF ICU nurse (3 as charge nurse) turned mission-driven solo founder chasing one specific problem: ICU shift-change handoff failure. His dominant work pattern is not "nurse" but **crisis-floor orchestrator** — the person who runs surge events, brokers physician-nurse conflict, and owns the cross-functional rails (bed control, pharmacy) when the system is breaking. His strongest edge by a wide margin is **Market**: he speaks fluent arcana of ICU handoff workflow, down to subtype-specific politics and Joint Commission sentinel-event statistics. His weakest is **Skill-Edge / Technical**: Codecademy-plus-tutorials puts him well below shipping capability. He is a nearly-pure Missionary (10/2) with a specific origin event — a 2022 handoff-miss patient death — anchoring the obsession. The binding constraint is a 6-month runway combined with a zero-to-one shape that needs a technical co-founder or contractor immediately.
